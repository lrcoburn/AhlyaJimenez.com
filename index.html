Hi, I'm Ahlya!

I'm a 16-year-old artist and junior in high school with a deep love for horses and the beauty of the natural world. Art has been part of my life for as long as I can remember, and I'm so excited to share it with you.

I specialize in equine and animal portraits — capturing the personality, spirit, and bond between horses and the people who love them. Whether you want a portrait of your beloved horse alone or a special piece of you together, I'd love to create something meaningful for you.
