About Me

My name is Ahlya, and I am an artist specializing in capturing the beauty and personality of horses. My creative journey began in early childhood, leading to a focus on detailed portraiture and equestrian subjects. Through a focus on texture and detail, my work captures the distinctive character of each horse, blending precision with artistic expression.

Every portrait I create is a unique representation of your horse's character and charm. Whether you're looking for a detailed study of your companion or a beautiful portrait featuring both you and your horse, I bring attention to detail and artistic passion to every commission.

My work combines acrylics, graphite, and mixed mediums to create personalized portraits that celebrate the bond between horse and owner.

How to Commission

1

Submit Photos

Send clear, well-lit photos of your horse or you with your horse. Multiple angles help me capture the perfect pose.

2

Choose Your Style

Select your preferred medium (graphite, colored pencil, acrylic, pencil) and size. We can discuss custom options.

3

Discuss Details

I'll reach out to discuss your vision, background preferences, and any special requests for your portrait.

4

Receive Your Art

Once complete, your custom portrait is carefully packaged and shipped to you. Each piece includes a certificate of authenticity.
